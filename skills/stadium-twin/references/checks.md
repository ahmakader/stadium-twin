# Self-check before publishing

Check at 1920×1080 and 390×844. Look at each screenshot; a test that only reads the console is not a check.

## Model
- [ ] Seen from above with north up, the stands are on the correct sides (no mirror image). Compare with a satellite view.
- [ ] Seat count printed by the build, next to the official capacity, with the reason for the difference.
- [ ] No floating or intersecting parts at stand corners; roof edges meet the towers.
- [ ] Lower-tier median C-value ≥ 60 mm. Report the upper tiers honestly if they come out lower.

## Booking
- [ ] "Find the best seats" returns results for 1, 2 and 6 tickets, and for the wheelchair filter. Every wheelchair result has a companion seat.
- [ ] "Sit here": the view looks at the pitch, the head in front is visible, and nothing clips the camera.
- [ ] Zones, sightline and price colouring each show a legend, and the colours match it.

## Safety
- [ ] Full-house egress finishes. Each stand's time is plausible against the 8-minute guide.
- [ ] A fire placed in a concourse spreads smoke along that concourse only. Exits in smoke turn red, and agents re-route.
- [ ] The dashboard clock, the counts and the chart agree with each other.

## Sections
- [ ] Stand, cross, long and plan cuts all show hatched cut faces, with no see-through gaps.
- [ ] Flat elevation reads like a drawing: no perspective, and labels are legible.
- [ ] The players' tunnel and each named room appear on the right stand and floor.
- [ ] The disclaimer that the interior is a reconstruction is visible in the panel.

## Page
- [ ] It loads with no console errors, and the loader finishes on a mid-range laptop.
- [ ] Phone layout: the panels collapse and nothing overflows horizontally.
- [ ] Trademarks: names only, with no crests or kits. The "not affiliated" line is visible.
