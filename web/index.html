    How this is calculated

    Movement. Every gangway, vomitory, concourse, stair and gate is a link in an escape network generated from the model. Walking speed follows the SFPE hydraulic method (S = k(1 − 0.266·D), k 1.40 level, 1.08 stepped), the method behind Pathfinder's SFPE mode. Link capacity uses the Green Guide rates of passage: 82 people per metre per minute on level routes and gates, 66 on stepped routes. Queues form where demand exceeds capacity and spill back when a link reaches 3.55 people/m².

    Design. Routes follow the modelled interior (see Sections): vomitories with stairs down to the concourses, the walkway and box passages behind each tier, and stair cores with lifts. Final gates and stair cores are sized so each stand can clear in 8 minutes at those rates (Green Guide capacity method). The real stadium's drawings are not public, so the interior is an informed reconstruction.

    Fire. t² growth with PD 7974-1 / NFPA α values, 500 kW/m² heat release per unit area, Heskestad flame height and plume, radiant heat from a point source (30 % radiated) against the 2.5 kW/m² tenability limit (10 kW/m² treated as impassable). Under a roof, a one-zone smoke reservoir fills from the plume and spills over the open roof front (CIBSE Guide E / BRE 368 spill method); in a concourse the smoke front follows Alpert's ceiling-jet speed. A route is smoke-logged when the clear height drops below 2.5 m.

    People. Full-time egress: some leave early, most within 4 minutes of the whistle. Fire: people who can see the flames move within about 30 s; everyone else starts after the alarm plus a pre-movement time (median 45 s), in the style of PD 7974-6. Wheelchair users go to the concourse refuges and use evacuation lifts 90 s after the alarm (BS 9999).

    A design-guideline model for exploring scenarios, not CFD (FDS) or a certified fire-engineering analysis.

    How the interior was modelled

    No construction drawings are public, so the inside is an informed reconstruction. The bowl itself is accurate to this model: every floor level is derived from it (the walkway and box level behind each tier, the concourse each upper-tier vomitory leads down to, and intermediate floors so no storey is taller than about 7.5 m).

    Rooms. Hospitality suites, the museum, the megastore, the dressing rooms and the players' tunnel (south-west corner) are placed using the club's published names, stands and floor areas. Their exact outlines are indicative; the room card shows both the modelled area and the club's figure.

    Stairs and lifts. Escape cores are sized with the same Green Guide capacity method as the safety simulation (8 minutes at 66 people per metre per minute on stairs, 10 % margin), and the simulation uses these stairs.

    Cut faces are drawn solid and hatched (poché). Measurements are in metres from the pitch level.
